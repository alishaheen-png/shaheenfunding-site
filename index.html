What we arrange

The right facility for the way you get paid.

Payroll and suppliers are due in 30 days. Clients pay in 60, 90 or 120. We find the facility that closes that gap, then line up the next one.

B2B services and contracting

Invoice discounting

A limit is approved once, say AED 1M, and held for you at the bank. For the next 3–4 years you draw on it against your invoices, repay and draw again as often as you need. Payroll and suppliers get paid on time while clients take 60–120 days to pay.

Food & beverage and retail

Funding against your card sales
