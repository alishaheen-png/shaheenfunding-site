Offers in 4 to 7 days, funds 2 to 4 weeks after you sign

  1. Day 1

    First call

    We go through how your cash moves: when your costs fall due, when your clients pay, and how much funding you need. By the end of the call you know whether we can help.

  2. Days 2 to 3

    File preparation

    We sign a mutual NDA, then you send four documents:

    • Trade licence
    • 12 months of bank statements
    • Last 4 VAT returns
    • Memorandum of Association (MOA)

    We turn them into one complete file, ready for the lenders.

  3. Days 4 to 7

    Offers from matched lenders

    We take your file to the banks and financiers that fit your case. Each lender sends its terms, so you can compare the offers side by side and choose which one to take forward.

  4. After you sign

    Submission and funding

    With your sign-off we submit the case, and the funds land 2 to 4 weeks after you sign. We handle every call, email and follow-up with the bank until the money is in your account.
