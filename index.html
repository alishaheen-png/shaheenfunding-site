What we arrange

The right facility for the problem you actually have.

We start from your cash cycle, not from a product list, then structure the facility that fixes it.

When the pain is paying out

Working capital finance

The lender pays your suppliers now and you repay on terms that match your collection cycle, so growth is not held back by cash tied up in stock and payroll.

When the pain is waiting to get paid

Invoice discounting

Release cash against invoices you have already issued. Strong customers on slow terms become an asset you can fund against, not a gap you have to bridge.

When the ambition is bigger

Long-term and high-value facilities

Larger, longer facilities through the banks, for expansion, capex and the moves a small platform cannot underwrite.
