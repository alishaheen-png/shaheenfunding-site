Funding advisory for UAE businesses

Shaheen Funding is a funding advisory business working with companies in the UAE.

What we do

We help businesses find and access the funding options available to them, working alongside each business to understand its situation and move the process forward.

How it works

Every business's funding needs are different, so we start by understanding the specific business in front of us — what it does, what it needs, and where it is in its journey.

From there, we help identify the funding paths that are actually relevant, and support the business as it works through them.
