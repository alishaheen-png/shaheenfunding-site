From first call to funds in 3 to 4 weeks

  1. Day 1

    First call

    We spend 20 minutes on how your cash moves.

  2. Days 2 to 3

    File preparation

    We sign a mutual NDA, then you send four documents:

    • Trade licence
    • 12 months of bank statements
    • Last 4 VAT returns
    • Memorandum of Association (MOA)

  3. Days 4 to 7

    Offers from matched lenders

    Each lender sends its terms, and you choose which offer to take forward.

  4. Weeks 3 to 4

    Submission and funding

    With your sign-off we submit the case, and the funds land 3 to 4 weeks after your first call.
