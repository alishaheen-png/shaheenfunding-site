What is an SME loan

An SME loan is money lent to a small or medium-sized business to cover a cash need such as stock, supplier bills, payroll or a new branch. The business repays it over an agreed period, with a charge for the money borrowed.

Each lender sets its own terms, so the same company can receive different offers from different banks. That is why we ask several of them at once, and you compare the offers side by side before you commit to one.
