Business loan requirements in UAE

Lenders ask for four documents: your trade licence, the last 12 months of bank statements, VAT returns for the last 4 quarters and your Memorandum of Association (MOA). We sign a mutual NDA first, then collect the file and anything extra a bank asks for.

The full list is on our page on the documents required for a business loan in UAE. Beyond the paperwork, we work with companies that have a timing problem: clients pay in 60, 90 or 120 days while payroll and supplier bills fall due in 30.
