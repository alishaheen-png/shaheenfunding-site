Business loan broker in Dubai, paid only after funds land

A business loan broker in Dubai takes your file to several lenders on your behalf and brings back offers for you to choose from. Shaheen Funding is a licensed advisory firm that does this for established UAE companies, with no upfront fees.

What is a business loan broker

A business loan broker is an intermediary who prepares your funding file, presents it to lenders and speaks to them for you. The lender makes the credit decision; the broker finds the lenders that fit and makes sure the file is seen.

We go straight to the heads of business banking and SME lending at 10+ UAE banks and financiers, so you never chase a junior banker.

Business finance consultant in UAE

A business finance consultant in UAE advises on which facility fits your cash flow and then helps you apply. With us the advice is the first 20-minute call, where we look at how your cash moves and match you to a facility.

The options are invoice discounting for B2B companies, funding against POS sales for food and beverage and retail, and funding for materials, stock and equipment for manufacturers and traders.

Corporate finance advisory in UAE

Corporate finance advisory in UAE means an independent firm that prepares your funding file and takes it to lenders for you. Shaheen Funding is a licensed advisory firm that takes one file to 10+ UAE banks and financiers and brings back several offers within 4 to 7 days.

Shaheen Funding charges no upfront fees. We earn a success fee only after the funds reach your account, so if the funds never arrive, you owe us no fee.
