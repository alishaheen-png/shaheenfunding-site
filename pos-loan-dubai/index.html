Who is POS funding for

POS funding is for food and beverage and retail businesses that already trade. A restaurant with a second site in mind, a cafe that needs a refit and a shop that needs to stock up before a busy season all fit.

What do you need to apply for a POS loan

You need four documents: your trade licence, 12 months of bank statements, VAT returns for the last 4 quarters and your Memorandum of Association (MOA). A mutual NDA is signed first, and the 20-minute first call sets the rest in motion.
