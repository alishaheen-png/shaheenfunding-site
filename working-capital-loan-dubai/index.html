Working capital loan in Dubai for the weeks before clients pay

A working capital loan in Dubai bridges the gap between paying your costs in 30 days and being paid in 60, 90 or 120. Shaheen Funding arranges it from 10+ banks and financiers, with offers usually under 1% a month.

How does a working capital loan work

A working capital loan is money lent to cover day-to-day costs such as payroll, suppliers and rent while cash from customers is still on its way. The business draws the funds, uses them to bridge the gap and repays the lender over an agreed period.

Ours is a bridge for a timing problem, so it suits companies that are healthy and have a cashflow gap.

How to get a working capital loan

Send us a short note on your business and what you need funding for, and we set up a first call. We prepare your file in days 2 to 3 and bring back offers from matched lenders within 4 to 7 days.

You choose an offer, we submit the case with your sign-off, and the funds land 2 to 4 weeks after you sign.

What can a working capital loan pay for

It pays the bills that fall due before your clients do: payroll and supplier bills, typically due in 30 days when clients pay in 60, 90 or 120. Funding for a new branch, stock or materials follows the same logic.

We take the file to the heads of business banking and SME lending at 10+ UAE banks and financiers, so you compare several offers and choose the one that suits your timing.

Is a working capital loan the right facility

It depends on how your business earns. B2B companies often do better with invoice discounting, food and beverage and retail businesses with funding against POS sales, and manufacturers with funding for materials, stock and equipment.
