Invoice discounting in UAE, drawn as often as you need

Invoice discounting in UAE lets a B2B company draw cash against its invoices instead of waiting 60, 90 or 120 days for clients to pay. We arrange the limit from 10+ banks and financiers, with offers usually under 1% a month.

Invoice financing UAE

Invoice financing in UAE means borrowing against the invoices you have issued, so cash arrives before your client pays. We arrange it for B2B companies in trading, contracting, services and distribution.

A limit is approved once, say AED 1M, and held for you at the bank. For the next 3 to 4 years you can draw against your invoices once or twenty times.

Invoice factoring UAE

Invoice factoring is a close cousin of discounting: the financier advances cash against your invoices and, in a typical factoring arrangement, also collects the payment from your client. With discounting, collection stays with you.

On the first call we look at how your cash moves and which structure suits your clients.

Bill discounting UAE

Bill discounting is another name for the same idea: a lender pays you part of the value of a bill or invoice now, and you repay it when your client pays. We arrange it as invoice discounting, with the limit held at the bank.

Invoice financing vs invoice discounting

The two terms overlap. Invoice financing is the broad name for borrowing against invoices, and invoice discounting is the form where you keep control of your own clients and collections. We arrange invoice discounting.

Receivables financing in UAE

Receivables financing in UAE is the wider family that invoice discounting belongs to: a lender advances cash against money your clients already owe you. We arrange it as a limit approved once and held at the bank, which you draw against invoices for 3 to 4 years.

Invoice finance in Dubai for contractors

Invoice finance in Dubai suits contractors and B2B suppliers whose clients pay in 60, 90 or 120 days. A limit is approved once, say AED 1M, and you draw against each invoice, repay when the client pays and draw again as often as you like.

We take the file to the heads of business banking and SME lending at 10+ UAE banks and financiers, and you choose between their offers.

How does invoice financing work

You issue an invoice, draw cash against it from your approved limit, and repay when your client pays. The limit stays in place, so you draw again on the next invoice as often as you like, or only once.

Offers arrive within 4 to 7 days, and funds land 3 to 4 weeks after your first call. We charge no upfront fees and earn a success fee only after the funds reach your account.
